Examples

Worked SALI calculations at different salary and price levels

These examples demonstrate how SALI works at various salary and Bitcoin price combinations. Each shows the formula applied step by step.

Example 1: Entry-Level Salary, Lower BTC Price

Annual Salary $50,000 USD
Bitcoin Price $25,000 USD
BTC Equivalent $50,000 ÷ $25,000 = 2.000000 BTC
SALI Result
200,000,000 sats/year

Calculation: 2.0 BTC × 100,000,000 = 200,000,000 sats

Example 2: Senior Salary, Mid-Range BTC Price

Annual Salary $120,000 USD
Bitcoin Price $60,000 USD
BTC Equivalent $120,000 ÷ $60,000 = 2.000000 BTC
SALI Result
200,000,000 sats/year

Calculation: 2.0 BTC × 100,000,000 = 200,000,000 sats

Note: Despite earning $70,000 more, the SALI is identical to Example 1. The salary-to-BTC ratio matters, not the absolute numbers.

Example 3: Mid-Level Salary, Higher BTC Price

Annual Salary $80,000 USD
Bitcoin Price $100,000 USD
BTC Equivalent $80,000 ÷ $100,000 = 0.800000 BTC
SALI Result
80,000,000 sats/year

Calculation: 0.8 BTC × 100,000,000 = 80,000,000 sats

Note: At a higher BTC price, the same labor commands fewer satoshis. This is the core insight SALI reveals.

Key Observations

  • SALI falls as BTC rises. If your salary stays flat and Bitcoin appreciates, your labor buys fewer sats each year.
  • Raises can be deceiving. A 10% salary increase paired with a 50% BTC price increase still results in a lower SALI.
  • The ratio is what matters. Examples 1 and 2 have identical SALI despite very different absolute values because their salary-to-BTC ratios are the same.
