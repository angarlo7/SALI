Why This Matters

Traditional salary increases can mask what's actually happening to your purchasing power. A 3% raise means little if the monetary base expands by 10%. SALI gives you a different lens: measuring labor in Bitcoin's fixed-supply unit.

Fiat salary increases can hide dilution. When you measure in satoshis, you see whether you're actually getting ahead or just treading water against monetary expansion.

SALI is measurement, not prediction. This tool doesn't forecast Bitcoin's price or tell you what to do. It simply converts your income into a different unit of account so you can observe the relationship between your labor and sound money.

This is not financial advice. SALI is an educational tool for those who want to understand their income from a Bitcoin-denominated perspective. It makes no claims about future prices or investment returns.

The Formula

SALI (sats/year) = (Annual Salary in USD ÷ BTC Price in USD) × 100,000,000

SALI converts your salary to its Bitcoin equivalent, then expresses it in satoshis (the smallest Bitcoin unit, 1 BTC = 100 million sats). This makes year-over-year comparisons intuitive.
